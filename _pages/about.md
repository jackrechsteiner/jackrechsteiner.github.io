I am a PhD student in the Linguistics program at the University of Pittsburgh working with [Dr. Villarreal](https://www.linguistics.pitt.edu/people/dan-villarreal). 
My research looks at how personal identity drives language change and imbues linguistic variation with social meaning, with a specific focus on queer, subcultural, and/or marginalized speakers. 
I'm interested in the ways that people navigate identity at macro-linguistic (interactional discourse) and micro-linguistic (phonetic variation) levels, particularly in relation to nonbinary or non-traditional genders.
